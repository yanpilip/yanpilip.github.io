About me

I'm Yan Pilipenko, a software engineer from Almaty, Kazakhstan, now based in Budapest.

I started out at Turan College, where I studied Information Systems and graduated with a GPA of 4.95 out of 5. During my studies there I got my first real work experience as a project manager intern at Expobest, coordinating a team of 60 interns and building small internal tools in PHP and MySQL. I then moved to Hungary and completed my BSc in Computer Science at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE). In my final year I worked as a research assistant at ELTE's Department of Numerical Analysis, building a PyTorch benchmarking framework for ECG signal quality assessment on more than 300,000 annotated segments.

That project pulled me towards machine learning, and in September 2026 I started my MSc in Computer Science with an Artificial Intelligence specialisation at ELTE. Alongside my studies, I work as a DevOps intern at 3 Sigma Studios. There I fix production bugs on a live trading platform, manage AWS infrastructure and Docker deployments, and built a CI/CD pipeline for an internal API. I also optimised the training of a production TrOCR model, making it three times faster. I speak Russian natively, English at C1 level and Japanese at B2, so I'm comfortable working in international teams.

After my MSc, I want to work as a machine learning engineer, building ML systems that run reliably in production rather than only in notebooks. Longer term, I'd like to work on projects that connect Europe and Japan, where both my engineering background and my languages are useful.
